# Awesome-Public-Meeting-Management

## Top Public Meeting Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Agenda Management, Board Packets, Minutes, Public Transparency Portals & Governance Workflows*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Public Meeting Management**. These systems help government clerks, school boards, councils, and organizations prepare agendas, distribute packets, record minutes, manage approvals, and publish materials for public transparency.



**Examples** include Granicus, BoardDocs, CivicClerk, iCompass, PrimeGov, Diligent Community, Novus Agenda, OnBoard, BoardPAC, and MinuteTraq (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full public-meeting and board-portal platforms are largely commercial. Practical open options exist for collaborative meeting minutes (**4Minitz**) and experimental governance tools. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Granicus](https://granicus.com/)**  

  Leading government agenda and meeting management platform supporting the full public meeting lifecycle, including agendas, packets, minutes, video, and public portals.



- **[BoardDocs](https://www.boarddocs.com/)**  

  Widely used board management and agenda platform for school districts, municipalities, and other public organizations.



- **[CivicClerk](https://www.civicplus.com/)**  

  CivicPlus agenda and meeting management solution designed for local government clerks and public meeting workflows.



- **[iCompass](https://www.icompasstech.com/)**  

  Meeting and agenda management software focused on public sector governance and transparency.



- **[PrimeGov](https://www.primegov.com/)**  

  Agenda, meeting, and legislative management platform for government bodies.



- **[Diligent Community](https://www.diligent.com/)**  

  Board and community governance platform (part of Diligent) supporting agenda preparation, packets, and meeting processes for public entities.



- **[Novus Agenda](https://www.novusagenda.com/)**  

  Agenda and meeting management software used by public agencies for structured meeting workflows.



- **[OnBoard](https://www.onboardmeetings.com/)**  

  Board portal and meeting management platform used by both public and private organizations.



- **[BoardPAC](https://www.boardpac.com/)**  

  Board meeting and document management solution supporting agendas, minutes, and secure collaboration.



- **[MinuteTraq](https://www.minutetraq.com/)**  

  Meeting minutes and agenda tracking software aimed at streamlining documentation and follow-up.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **4Minitz** for internal or smaller-scale collaborative minute-taking where full public-meeting compliance features are not required.

- Publishing agendas and minutes via open static-site or document tools for basic transparency.

- Combining open minute-taking with commercial agenda and packet platforms.

- Accepting that structured public agenda workflows, formal packet distribution, video integration, ADA-compliant portals, and government-grade audit trails still favor commercial platforms (Granicus, BoardDocs, CivicClerk, Diligent Community, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on privacy-preserving minute-taking and public publication rather than replacing full clerk-centric systems.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Prepare agendas in collaborative docs or 4Minitz → distribute packets via open document tools → capture minutes live → track action items → publish to a public portal. Suitable for smaller boards or internal meetings. Most public agencies continue to rely on commercial meeting management platforms designed for statutory requirements, transparency mandates, and clerk workflows.
